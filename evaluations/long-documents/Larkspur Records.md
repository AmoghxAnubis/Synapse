## Records practice 1

Clerks label each records bundle with its region and review cycle. They compare the inventory with the last handover sheet, record any missing bundle, and retain the reviewer signature. This inventory procedure does not set a new retention rule. Observed differences are documented before the next shift takes responsibility.
