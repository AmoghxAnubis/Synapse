# Compound citation continuation — 11 October 2026

The earlier [PDF/DOCX/Markdown holdout](SECOND_HOLDOUT.md) supplied both passages for an unnamed compound question, but two of three repeated generated answers cited only one source. The new guard records the lead passage for each retrieved clause. For a two-clause question with different lead passages, it buffers the answer, checks for both citation numbers, retries once with explicit per-part citation instructions, and withholds a still-uncited answer. Ordinary questions retain token streaming. This is a citation-presence safeguard; it does not judge whether a cited passage actually supports every claim.

The local three-repeat development screen used the unchanged Cedar fixtures and `llama3:latest`. All three compound answers stated both facts and cited both sources, versus one of three before the guard. The buffered compound answers began output after 4.404, 6.372 and 5.541 seconds; the entire answer arrived as one token event. This increases perceived first-token latency and makes the earlier Q02 streaming baseline inapplicable to this path. The screening script also needed its missing `json` and `ctypes` imports before it could run. Its new output is kept in ignored `.tmp/q01-citation-guard.json`.
